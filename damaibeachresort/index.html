Santubong Peninsula • Sarawak

Damai Beach Resort

"Where rainforest meets the South China Sea."

Explore Chalets & Rooms
The Sanctuary

Nestled on 90 Acres of Primeval Coastal Rainforest

Damai Beach Resort is Sarawak's premier beachfront resort destination. Set against the mythical backdrop of Mount Santubong, our resort offers authentic Malaysian timber chalets, pristine golden sand coves, and two swimming pools including our clifftop horizon pool overlooking the sea.

Awaken to the calls of hornbills, hike lush jungle trails, explore the neighboring Sarawak Cultural Village, or savor fresh seafood under the Borneo stars.
