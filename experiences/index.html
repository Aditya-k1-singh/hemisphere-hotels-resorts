From misty primeval rainforest trails and restorative coastal spa rituals to UNESCO gastronomic journeys along the Sarawak River.

Coastal & Wilderness

Rainforest & Beach Sanctuary

Santubong is where mythical legends meet untouched nature. Nestled between the ancient slopes of Mount Santubong and the warm waters of the South China Sea, our coastal properties offer individual timber chalets, secluded golden beaches, and guided eco-trekking through Borneo's primeval canopy.

Featured Properties:
Damai Beach Resort • Damai Lagoon Resort

Restorative Healing

Eden Damai Wellness Rituals

Borneo's indigenous flora holds ancient healing secrets. At Eden Damai Wellness, therapeutic hydrotherapy pools, oceanfront yoga decks, and private treatment pavilions create a haven of calm. Surrender to signature massages infused with wild ginger, cold-pressed virgin coconut oil, and highland mineral salts.

Featured Sanctuary:
Damai Lagoon Resort • Eden Spa Sanctuary
Discover Eden Damai
UNESCO Gastronomy

Heritage Flavors & Skyline Dining

As Malaysia's first UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy, Kuching's culinary soul is celebrated across our portfolio. From Halal Cantonese and Macanese roast duck at Grand Margherita's Macau Restaurant to 18th-floor rooftop Wagyu fine dining at Suasana on 18, our chefs honor both ancient recipes and modern innovation.

Featured Venues:
Macau Restaurant • Suasana on 18 • Cafe Satang • River Palace
View Dining Packages
Cultural Odyssey

Living Heritage of Sarawak

From the doorsteps of our riverfront city hotels, step directly into centuries of history along the Kuching Waterfront promenade, colonial Fort Margherita, and the world-class Borneo Cultures Museum. At Santubong, explore indigenous architectural longhouses at the Sarawak Cultural Village.

Gateway Addresses:
Grand Margherita Hotel • Riverside Majestic Puteri & Astana
