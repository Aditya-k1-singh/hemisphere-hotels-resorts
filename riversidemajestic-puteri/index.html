The 5-Star Landmark

The Premier Address for Regional Summits & Grand Stays

Rising majestically above the scenic Sarawak River, the Puteri Wing of Riverside Majestic Hotel stands as the symbol of 5-star sophistication in Sarawak. Adjoining the Riverside Shopping Complex and looking across to Fort Margherita, it combines understated grandeur, spacious club accommodations, and the celebrated Puteri Grand Ballroom capable of hosting up to 1,200 delegates.

Indulge in Cantonese fine dining at River Palace, host diplomatic conferences with cutting-edge hybrid AV facilities, or unwind beside our riverside swimming pool with sunset views.
