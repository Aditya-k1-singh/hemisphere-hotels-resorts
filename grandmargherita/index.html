Grand Margherita Hotel

"An elegant address on the Sarawak River."

Explore City & River Rooms
The Heritage Address

Where the Sarawak River Meets the City

Situated in the heart of Kuching's business, shopping, and cultural district, Grand Margherita Hotel holds the proud distinction of being Sarawak's first international 4-star city hotel. Commanding panoramic views of the Sarawak River and historic Astana Palace, it combines nostalgic grandeur with modern executive amenities.

Step directly out to the vibrant Kuching Waterfront promenade, indulge in celebrated Macanese roast meats at Macau Restaurant, or host grand galas in our column-free 1,000-delegate Grand Ballroom.
