The Boutique Tower

Elevated Urban Luxury & Dramatic Skyline Horizons

The Astana Wing represents Hemisphere's chic, contemporary expression of boutique hospitality. Elevated high above the Sarawak River, this striking glass tower features floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass, sleek designer bathrooms with freestanding soaking tubs, and the celebrated Suasana on 18 rooftop restaurant and sky lounge.

Designed for design-conscious executives and leisure travelers who appreciate modern aesthetics, intelligent room technology, and private club privileges overlooking the city.
