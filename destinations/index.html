Kuching Waterfront • Santubong Peninsula • Sarawak

Borneo Odyssey

Where the River Meets the Rainforest

Sarawak is an intoxicating tapestry of primordial wilderness, mythical mountains, living indigenous culture, and refined riverfront elegance.

Santubong Peninsula

The Mythical Slopes of Santubong

Rising like a proud guardian from the South China Sea, Mount Santubong is steeped in local folklore—the legendary mountain princess Princess Santubong whose spirit protects the coast. Today, the peninsula provides a serene coastal refuge just 45 minutes from Kuching city center.

Hike ancient jungle trails inhabited by hornbills, relax on secluded beaches, or play 18 holes at the Arnold Palmer-designed Damai Golf Club nestled beside the sea.

View Coastal Resorts
The Capital

Kuching Waterfront: The River City

Few Asian capitals possess the intimate, walkable grace of Kuching. The scenic Sarawak River flows past historical 19th-century colonial landmarks, the romantic white towers of Fort Margherita, the stately Astana Palace, and the iconic Darul Hana Bridge.

Wander through Carpenter Street's heritage shophouses, explore the magnificent Borneo Cultures Museum—Southeast Asia's second largest—and savor legendary bowls of piping hot Sarawak Laksa.

View Riverfront Hotels
Living Heritage

Sarawak Cultural Village

Located a brief 5-minute stroll from Damai Beach Resort, the acclaimed "Living Museum" showcases authentic architectural dwellings and traditions of Sarawak's major ethnic communities: Iban, Bidayuh, Orang Ulu, Penan, Melanau, Malay, and Chinese.

Witness traditional weave crafting, taste bamboo rice, and marvel at award-winning cultural music and dance performances inside the air-conditioned theatre.
